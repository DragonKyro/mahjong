# Credits

## Tile artwork

The SVG mahjong tiles in [public/tiles/](public/tiles/) come from the
**[FluffyStuff/riichi-mahjong-tiles](https://github.com/FluffyStuff/riichi-mahjong-tiles)**
project, released into the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The "Regular"
variant is used; Dora (red-five) variants and the unused tile back/front
templates were not copied.

The naming convention is FluffyStuff's: `Man1`–`Man9` (萬), `Pin1`–`Pin9` (筒),
`Sou1`–`Sou9` (索), `Ton`/`Nan`/`Shaa`/`Pei` (東南西北), `Chun`/`Hatsu`/`Haku`
(中發白), plus `Back` and `Front`. Cantonese flowers and seasons (花/季) are not
in the FluffyStuff set and are currently rendered with a text fallback.
